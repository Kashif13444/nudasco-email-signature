How to use your signature
  1. Enter your full name, designation, mobile number and NUDASCO email address in the fields on the left.
  2. Check the live preview to make sure your details are correct.
  3. Click Copy for Outlook.
  4. Open New Outlook and go to Settings → Accounts → Signatures.
  5. Create a new signature, give it a name (for example, NUDASCO), then paste the copied signature into the signature editor.
  6. Click Save. Choose the signature for new messages and replies/forwards if required.
  7. Send yourself a test email and check that the logo, ISO badges and contact details display correctly.

Tip: The company branding, website, office address and landline are fixed. Only the four employee fields are editable. If copying does not work in your browser, click Download HTML and open the downloaded file, then copy the signature into Outlook.
